About

A full-stack team, six years in.

Zention Systems is a full-stack software development team with 6 years of industry experience building web and mobile products end to end. We work across the stack — from React and Vue on the frontend to Node, .NET, Go, and PHP on the backend — and ship for both web and mobile.

Our flagship product, ZERP, is an ERP system purpose-built for vehicle spare parts importers and retailers, handling everything from inventory and procurement to sales operations for businesses in that space. It reflects how we work: understanding a specific industry's real operational pain points, then building software that fits the way that business actually runs — not a generic template forced onto it.

We're currently operating as a startup, taking on new projects while continuing to grow ZERP, and we bring the same full-stack, product-minded approach to every engagement.
